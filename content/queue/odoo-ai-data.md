Most "AI for ERP" demos skip the part that actually decides whether we can ship it.

The master data.

If product names are inconsistent, vendors are duplicated, and units of measure are a rumour, an assistant will sound confident and still be wrong. Odoo will not save you from that. A model on top of Odoo will amplify it.

I would rather spend a week cleaning records than put our name on a chatbot that invents a stock move.

The unsexy work is the product. We do not get to skip it because a demo looked good on Thursday.

What is the messiest data problem you still trip over in Odoo?
