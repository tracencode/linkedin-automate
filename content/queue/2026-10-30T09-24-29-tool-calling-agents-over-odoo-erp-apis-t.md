The recent wave of open-source AI tools like Allos and EZClaw has stirred excitement. They promise easy deployment of AI agents, but I see a need for caution.

On the surface, these tools appear to streamline processes, making it easier to connect AI agents with Odoo/ERP APIs. However, many fail to account for the messy realities of production. Last week, we had a project where a new tool-calling agent looked promising in theory. It handled initial requests without issue, but when we tested it with real data, the breakdowns began.

Misconfigured endpoints. Data mismatches. Unforeseen latency issues. Each error revealed gaps that weren’t visible in the sandbox. The excitement of rapid deployment faded quickly when we saw the operational hurdles.

Before I trust any new integration, I interrogate the failure modes that matter. What happens when the data isn't clean? What are the limits of error handling?

It reminds me that not every shiny tool will survive the demands of a live environment. What challenges have you faced with new AI tools in production?
