Deploying AI agents without operational checks is a risky move.

The recent buzz around tools like Allos and EZClaw promises quick deployments of AI agents over Odoo and ERP systems. While the appeal of fast integration is tempting, I’ve seen teams rush into it, only to face significant setbacks.
In real-world operations, these tools can create failure points that lead to data discrepancies and operational chaos. The integration with ERP APIs isn't as straightforward as it seems. I've had to recalibrate expectations after teams realized that production data doesn’t mirror demo environments.
Caution is crucial. I prioritize thorough testing and validation before committing to these new tools. It's about making sure they align with actual workflows and do not just look good on paper. Quick wins can sometimes mask deeper flaws that reveal themselves only after deployment.
