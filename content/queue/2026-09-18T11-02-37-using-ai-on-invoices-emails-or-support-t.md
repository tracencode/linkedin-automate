I've been exploring how AI can help manage invoices within Odoo. The goal is to streamline processes, reduce manual errors, and free up time for the team.

We set up an AI system that scans incoming invoices, extracting data into Odoo. It seemed straightforward. The AI would read the invoice, pull out key details, and populate the fields automatically.

However, we hit a snag. The variance in invoice formats was staggering. Different vendors had their own styles, making it difficult for the AI to interpret data accurately. Instead of saving time, we ended up spending hours correcting mistakes and training the AI.

This experience taught me that while AI can enhance workflows, it still requires a strong foundation in data quality. If the input isn't reliable, the output won't be either. We shifted our focus to cleaning up our master data before relying heavily on AI. It's an ongoing process, but essential for meaningful automation.
