The latest excitement around LLM-agnostic agents has me reflecting on development choices. Tools like Allos and OpenClaw promise a quick setup, but there’s a hidden cost to consider. I've seen firsthand how the allure of rapid deployment can lead teams to overlook the nuances of their existing systems.

When a customer asked us to customize Odoo for a specific function, I pushed back on the idea of building a new module. The existing configurations could cover their needs with adjustments. Custom modules can easily introduce maintenance headaches when updates roll out.

This is where applied AI comes into play. If the AI agent can fit within the current Odoo framework, it’s less risky. But if it requires extensive custom work, the long-term implications could outweigh the benefits. Fitting AI into existing workflows is often more sustainable than creating bespoke solutions that only work until the next update.

Have you faced a similar decision about customizing versus configuring? What trade-offs did you weigh?
