Choosing between configuring Odoo and developing a custom module can be tricky. I've faced this dilemma multiple times during implementations. A few months back, a client wanted very specific functionality that was not available out of the box. They asked for a custom module to handle unique inventory tracking requirements.

At first glance, it seemed necessary. But after digging into their request, we found that many of their needs could be met by proper configuration of existing modules. By using Odoo's built-in features, we could save them time and money while ensuring easier future upgrades. Custom modules can lead to technical debt and maintenance challenges.

The right approach often depends on the underlying business processes. If the requirement is common across industries, configuration typically works best. Custom modules should be reserved for unique cases where no alternative exists.

In the end, we opted for configuration, which not only addressed the client's needs but also left them with a cleaner system. Have you faced a similar situation where a potential custom build turned out to be better served by configuration?
