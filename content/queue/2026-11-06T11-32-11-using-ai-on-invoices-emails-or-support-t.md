There’s a fine line between automating and complicating workflows with AI.

Recent tools like EZClaw make it easy to deploy AI agents for tasks like invoice processing. However, I’ve seen firsthand how rushing into these solutions can lead to chaos. A client once implemented an automated email response system, only to find that it mismanaged critical ticket escalations. Instead of streamlining operations, it created bottlenecks.
The promise of hands-free processing is tempting but often overlooks the nuances of real business processes. Not every task should be automated. Some require human oversight, especially in environments where response quality matters. It's vital to assess which operations genuinely benefit from AI integration and which would be better handled manually. The goal should always be to enhance productivity, not add layers of complexity.
