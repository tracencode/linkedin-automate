One of the most common missteps in Odoo implementations is rushing to install a module. There’s often a temptation to grab the shiny new tool without fully understanding the implications.

I recently had a client who insisted on installing a popular inventory management module. It looked promising on the surface. But once it was live, we hit a wall. The module wasn’t compatible with their existing workflows. Features that were supposed to streamline operations ended up creating additional complexity.

We spent more time fixing the fallout than if we had taken a moment to assess their unique needs. The team ended up frustrated, and the initial excitement was replaced with confusion.

Before installing any module, I now recommend a rigorous evaluation against actual business processes. It’s critical to ensure that what looks good on paper will truly deliver value in practice.
