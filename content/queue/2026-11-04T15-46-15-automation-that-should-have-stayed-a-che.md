In the race to adopt new AI tools, a checklist approach often gets overlooked.

Tools like EZClaw might promise a quick integration of agents into workflows, but not all tasks are suitable for automation.
I remember a project where a team jumped at the chance to automate their support ticket handling with an AI-driven agent. They envisioned cutting down response times without fully considering the nuances of their processes. What ensued was a chaotic blend of misrouted tickets and frustrated users.
This experience highlighted that not every aspect of operations is ready for AI intervention. Tasks still require human judgment and oversight. When we prioritize speed over scrutiny, it can lead to failures that disrupt workflows rather than enhance them.
As we explore the potential of AI agents, let’s remember: some processes should remain manual and systematic. Have you experienced automation fails that would have been better managed with a checklist?
