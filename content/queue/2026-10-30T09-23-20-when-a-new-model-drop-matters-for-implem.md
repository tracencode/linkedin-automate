A recent spike in chatter about open-source AI agents has raised eyebrows. Tools like Allos and OpenClaw promise rapid deployment and flexibility. Sounds exciting. But here's the catch: excitement doesn't always translate to production readiness.

As a practitioner, I see too many teams excited about shiny new models without considering their operational landscape. A model drop may look impressive in demos, but integrating it into existing workflows? Entirely different game. We’ve had cases where a new LLM caused more headaches than solutions, especially with messy master data.

When evaluating a new model, I focus on three core questions:
What data will it work with?
How will it integrate with current systems?
And most importantly, will it really improve our performance?

Without answers, we risk chasing noise rather than value. Let’s invest our time in tools that enhance the existing operations, not create new problems.
