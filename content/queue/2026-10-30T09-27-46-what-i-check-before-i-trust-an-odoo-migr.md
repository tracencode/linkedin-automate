With the recent buzz around open-source AI agents, I’m reminded of the complexities behind migrations that integrate these new tools into existing systems. Teams often get caught up in the allure of quick deployment without fully assessing the impact on operational stability.

Before approving any Odoo migration that also shifts AI features, I focus on three critical checks. First, I ensure data integrity. Clean data is non-negotiable; if the master data is dirty, AI features will struggle or fail outright. Second, I review the workflow design. How will the new AI features interact with existing processes? A well-mapped workflow is essential for success. Finally, I assess user training and adoption strategies. New technologies won’t deliver value if the users aren’t equipped to leverage them effectively.

These steps might seem tedious, but they’re crucial for ensuring smooth transitions and maintaining trust in the system. Have you encountered challenges with AI migrations that disrupted operations?
