Dirty master data undermines even the best AI workflows. I encountered this in a recent project where a client was eager to implement an AI-driven invoice processing tool. They focused on the glitzy features, confident it would solve their problems with efficiency and accuracy. But when we dived into the data, we uncovered inconsistencies that would derail the entire operation. Vendor names were misspelled, invoice formats varied wildly, and duplicate entries were rampant. The AI assistant, while sophisticated, couldn't compensate for this chaos.

After several frustrating attempts to integrate the AI tool, we pivoted back to the basics: cleaning the master data. It was a tedious process, but it became clear that without this foundational step, we would only be automating errors. This experience reinforced the importance of rigorous data quality checks before deploying any AI solution in an ERP context. It’s a reminder that clever tech is only as good as the data it’s fed.

How do you ensure data quality before launching an AI project?
