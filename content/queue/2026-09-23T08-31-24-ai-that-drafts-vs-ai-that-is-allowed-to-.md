We've all seen AI that drafts content. It's great for generating ideas or creating templates, but there's a hard line when it comes to allowing it to write directly into production data. Recently, we faced a scenario where a customer wanted to automate responses to support tickets using AI. The drafting AI could produce coherent replies, but when we considered letting it post directly to the ticketing system, I pushed back.

Here’s why: drafting doesn’t account for the nuances of each ticket. An AI can misinterpret context or misapply tone. It might suggest a resolution that doesn't fit due to missing background knowledge. I insisted we keep a manual review step before any AI-generated reply hit the system.

In this case, it was about risk management. The stakes are high when a customer is relying on us for accurate and consistent communication. Automating too freely can lead to errors that damage trust.

Are you letting your AI write directly into production, or do you have a review process? What’s your experience?
