The recent wave of open-source AI tools, particularly those claiming easy deployment of AI agents, brings a double-edged sword. While they promise to revolutionize workflows, I've seen too many examples where demo AI copilots fall short in real operations.

In warehouses, the stakes are high. We can’t afford a system that works well in a controlled environment but fails when real data and real tasks come into play. A demo AI copilot might look impressive, but if it can’t handle messy inputs—like inconsistent item descriptions or unexpected inventory anomalies—it won’t earn trust from the team on the ground.

I refuse to ship AI solutions that haven’t gone through rigorous testing under actual operational stress. For instance, we recently piloted an AI copilot for inventory management. It performed flawlessly on demo data but stumbled when tackling our clients' diverse SKU formats.

The key takeaway? A copilot must be tailored to the specific challenges faced in production, not just the flashy features that shine in demos. How do you ensure your AI tools bridge the gap between demo and deployment?
