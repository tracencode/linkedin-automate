The rush to deploy AI often overlooks critical data quality.

With the recent buzz around tools like EZClaw, it's tempting to think that quick integrations can solve all our problems. But from experience, I know that deploying AI without addressing data quality can lead to chaos. One of our clients eagerly adopted an AI-driven document processing solution, only to struggle with messy scans and inconsistent invoice formats. The AI faltered when it encountered multilingual invoices embedded with stamps and handwritten notes.
If we want our AI to work in production, we have to ensure our input data is clean and consistent. A powerful AI tool is rendered useless if it can't handle the real-world complexities we face daily. Our focus should be on the quality of the documents we feed into these systems, or we risk setting ourselves up for failure.
