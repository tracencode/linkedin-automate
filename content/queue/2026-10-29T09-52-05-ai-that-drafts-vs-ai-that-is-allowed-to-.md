I've been asked if AI should draft content or be allowed to write directly into production data. It's a critical distinction.

In a recent project, we implemented an AI tool designed to assist with generating customer communications. The idea was to boost productivity by drafting emails and support responses.

However, we faced pushback on allowing AI to send messages without human review. The concern? Misinterpretations or errors could impact customer relationships.

So, we opted for a hybrid approach. The AI drafts, but a human reviews and approves before it goes out. This keeps quality in check while still leveraging automation.

Choosing to restrict AI's role to drafting maintains accountability. It prevents potential damage from hasty miscommunications.

This conversation often leads to a broader question: Where should we draw the line with AI in operations? How do you balance efficiency with accuracy?
