There’s a growing trend of AI tools promising seamless automation. A recent spike in open-source agents like Allos and EZClaw highlights this. They look great on paper, but here's where caution is needed.

I've seen teams rush to automate processes without evaluating their operational impact. Automation should simplify workflows, not complicate them with new errors. In my experience, many tasks are better off on a checklist than fully automated.

For instance, I worked with a customer who wanted to automate inventory checks using a new AI tool. We found that their existing manual processes, while slow, were actually more reliable in navigating discrepancies. The AI tool would have added unnecessary complexity and risk.

As practitioners, we need to remember that not every workflow should be automated. Sometimes, keeping a human in the loop is essential for success.
