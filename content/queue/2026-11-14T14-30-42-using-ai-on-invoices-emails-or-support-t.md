The reality of implementing AI for invoices or support tickets often clashes with expectations.

Teams are excited to automate processes, but they overlook the complexities of their operational data. I recently guided a client through automating invoice processing. The AI worked well in the lab with pristine data. But when it faced messy, inconsistent inputs in production, it faltered. We had to step back and address the data quality issues first. In many cases, automation shines when paired with clean, structured data. If you skip this step, you risk deploying a solution that looks good on paper but crumbles in practice. This is a crucial lesson for anyone considering AI in their ERP workflows. Are we truly ready to tackle our dirty data before we jump into automation?
