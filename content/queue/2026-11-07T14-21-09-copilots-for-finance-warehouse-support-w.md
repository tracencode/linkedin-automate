The appeal of AI copilots for warehouse or finance operations can be tempting.

Tools like EZClaw offer rapid deployment, but I've learned the hard way that not everything should be automated. I refuse to automate tasks that rely heavily on nuanced judgment or human insight. In finance, for instance, decisions based on context can make or break a month-end close. Automating those could lead to compliance issues or incorrect reporting. In a recent project, we encountered a situation where an AI copilot was set to make inventory decisions. It faltered under pressure, missing critical context that only a human could provide. This isn't about resisting innovation; it's about recognizing the limits of technology in high-stakes environments. What are your thoughts on the risks of over-automation in operational roles?
