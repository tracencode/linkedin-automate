We recently tackled a seemingly minor Odoo customization for a client. They needed a simple change in their sales order workflow, just a field modification to streamline data entry. It appeared straightforward. But as we dug deeper, the implications unfolded.

Adjusting the sales order structure meant re-evaluating dependencies across multiple modules. Integrations with inventory and accounting systems were affected. Data validation rules had to be updated to ensure compliance. What started as a small tweak snowballed into a larger undertaking, requiring additional QA and user training. The lesson? Small changes can trigger a cascade of unforeseen challenges within your ERP.

Every customization, no matter how trivial it seems, deserves thorough analysis. It’s not about the immediate request; it’s about understanding the underlying architecture and how every piece fits together.
