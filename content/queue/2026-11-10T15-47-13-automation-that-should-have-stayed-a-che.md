The excitement around tools like EZClaw can lead teams into risky territory.

Rapid deployment sounds great on paper, but I’ve seen firsthand how automation can fail when it skips critical evaluations. I once worked with a client eager to automate their support ticket handling. They believed AI could manage nuanced customer interactions better than manual review. The result? Misunderstandings piled up, customer satisfaction dropped, and the team lost trust in the AI system.
In production, the complexity of human interactions often requires a level of judgment that automation simply can't replicate. If we rush to automate without a thorough understanding of the task at hand, we risk turning our workflows into chaos. Sometimes, a checklist is all we need to avoid automation pitfalls.
