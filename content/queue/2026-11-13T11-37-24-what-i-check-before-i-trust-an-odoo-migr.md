When migrating to Odoo while introducing AI features, the stakes are high.

I've learned that a successful migration hinges on a few critical checks. First, I inspect the integrity of the data being migrated. Dirty master data can ruin an AI workflow before it even begins. Second, I evaluate the compatibility of existing processes with the new AI models. Just because a model performs well in isolation doesn't mean it will integrate seamlessly with operational workflows. Third, I run a series of simulations. This helps catch potential issues that wouldn't show up during standard testing. I've seen unexpected failures emerge only after going live, which reinforces the importance of thorough validation. Each of these steps is essential to ensure that both Odoo and any AI features will work reliably in the real world. After all, if a migration isn't solid, it can jeopardize the entire system, AI included. What checks do you prioritize when merging AI with existing systems?
