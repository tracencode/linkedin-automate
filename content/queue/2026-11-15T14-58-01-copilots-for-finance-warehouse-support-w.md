Not every task should fall into the hands of a copilot.

Systems can look efficient on paper, but when deployed, they reveal their flaws. We often discuss the benefits of AI in operational settings, particularly in finance and support roles. However, I draw the line at automating decision-making processes that require human judgment.
For instance, a finance team recently asked for an AI copilot to manage expense approvals. While the AI can analyze patterns, it lacks the nuance required for exceptions. Automating this could lead to errors that cost the company. The same applies to support ticket handling. It’s tempting to think a copilot can manage these interactions, but understanding context and emotions is essential.
Instead, I focus on augmenting human capabilities. I empower teams by providing AI tools that assist, not replace, their decision-making. This approach respects the value of human insight and ensures that our systems can handle the complexities of real-world operations without compromising integrity.
What tasks do you think should always involve human oversight?
