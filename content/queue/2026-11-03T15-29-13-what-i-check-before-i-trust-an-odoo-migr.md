The rush to deploy AI agents can obscure vital operational checks.

Tools like Allos and EZClaw promise rapid integration with Odoo and ERP systems, making them sound appealing for teams looking for efficiency. But I've seen too many organizations dive in without due diligence. When migrating Odoo systems to include AI features, I always check the integrity of existing data and workflows. If there's dirty master data, even the smartest AI won't save you from chaos. I prefer to pause and ensure that the migration plan addresses potential pitfalls, such as data quality issues, process changes, and user adoption. Rushing ahead can lead to broken workflows that drag teams back into manual processes. The cost of a mismanaged migration is often higher than taking the time to lay a solid foundation. What checks do you insist on before moving to AI-enhanced migrations?
