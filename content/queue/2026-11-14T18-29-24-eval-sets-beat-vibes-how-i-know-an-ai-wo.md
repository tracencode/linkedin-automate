Operational readiness for AI workflows hinges on evaluation sets, not just good vibes.

Each project demands rigorous testing against real-world data before deployment. I insist on this because I’ve learned the hard way that a model that performs well in theory can flop under actual conditions. Without a structured eval set, it’s easy to misjudge the potential impact on operations.
I recall a recent implementation where we neglected to stress-test the AI with dirty master data. The model functioned smoothly in the lab but stumbled in production. That experience reinforced my commitment to rigorous evaluation.
Trusting the output of an AI model shouldn't be based on gut feelings. It needs to be backed by solid data and proven performance metrics. If your workflow isn't tested thoroughly, it’s a gamble. How have you validated your AI workflows before going live?
