Many teams get caught in the excitement of AI drafting capabilities.

It’s easy to see how impressive the technology can be in controlled environments. But the reality is harsher in production. AI that drafts offers potential, but it can lead to significant risks if not tightly controlled. I've seen AI generate outputs that sound right but are not validated against the core data structure.
When allowing an AI to write directly into production data, the stakes rise. You need checks and balances to prevent errors that can ripple through your operations. Data cleanliness is paramount. A model that performs well on pristine data can falter with the messy realities we face daily. I've had to intervene when clients hastily integrated drafting AI without a proper review process. The cost of that oversight? Disruption, downtime, and rework.
How do you ensure that AI outputs are validated before they touch your production systems?
