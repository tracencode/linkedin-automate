The latest excitement around tools like EZClaw promises rapid deployment of AI agents.

However, these solutions often gloss over a critical reality: dirty master data. In my experience, the allure of an impressive AI workflow can quickly fade when the underlying data quality is compromised. I once worked with a team eager to automate their invoice processing. They implemented an AI agent in record time, but the data they fed it was riddled with inaccuracies. Instead of streamlining operations, they faced significant backlogs due to incorrect entries and rework. AI can enhance productivity, but it can’t compensate for poor data practices. Before jumping into the latest tools, ensure your foundational data is clean and reliable. Otherwise, you'll find that no AI, no matter how clever, can overcome the chaos of dirty data.
