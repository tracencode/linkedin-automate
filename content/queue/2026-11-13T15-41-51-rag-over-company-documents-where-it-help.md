RAG systems can be transformative, but they often hit a wall with real-world data.

I’ve noticed that teams expect seamless results, assuming the AI can sift through all company documents effortlessly.
However, when it comes to messy documents, the hallucinations start. AI that pulls data from poorly structured or ambiguous texts can easily misinterpret context or provide irrelevant information. I once evaluated a system that claimed to offer a comprehensive view of project documents. Under the surface, it struggled with inconsistent terminology and formats.
The potential is there, but the implementation must account for real-world nuances. Preparing your documents isn’t just a box to check. It’s essential for the success of any RAG approach. How do you ensure your data quality before applying AI?
