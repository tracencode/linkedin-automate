Dirty master data undermines even the best AI workflows. I encountered this in a recent project where a client wanted to implement an AI-driven invoice processing tool. They were captivated by the promise of efficiency and accuracy. But we had to pull back.

Before we even considered deploying the AI, we did a thorough review of their data. It was a mess—duplicate vendors, inconsistent product names, and missing values. We spent weeks cleaning it up.

This wasn't just a checklist item. It was essential. The AI tool would only be as good as the data feeding it. If the foundation is shaky, the results will be too.

After the cleanup, the AI performed remarkably well. It automated invoice processing and reduced errors significantly. But getting there required discipline and a clear understanding of the critical role that clean data plays.

The lesson? Effective AI deployment in ERP relies more on the quality of your master data than on the sophistication of the technology itself. What’s your experience with data quality impacting AI implementations?
