Recent tools like EZClaw make it sound simple to deploy AI agents.

However, there’s a significant gap between what works in a demo and what’s practical for day-to-day operations. I've seen teams excited about deploying these shiny new tools, only to realize they don't fit the grittiness of real-world workflows.
A warehouse team needs AI that understands nuances: it should process complicated orders and adapt to unexpected scenarios without misfires. In one instance, a client rushed to implement an AI copilot for inventory management without considering the specific challenges of their operation. They soon faced a barrage of errors that came from an overly simplified understanding of their processes.
The lesson here is clear: if a tool can't handle the complexities of real operations, it's just a demo. What’s your experience with deploying AI tools in operational settings?
