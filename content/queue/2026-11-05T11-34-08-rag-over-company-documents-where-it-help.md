Many teams underestimate the hallucination risk in RAG models.

With the recent emergence of tools like Allos and EZClaw, there's a surge in excitement around RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) applications. These can be a boon for processing company documents, especially when they leverage vast datasets. However, I’ve seen firsthand how quickly things can go sideways.
Just last week, I worked with a client eager to implement RAG to pull insights from their financial documents. While the model provided impressive responses during testing, it produced inaccuracies when faced with real-world data inconsistencies. Hallucinations crept in, leading to confusion and loss of trust among users.
The lesson here is clear: RAG can enhance workflows, but it demands rigorous validation against live documents. Rushing to deploy without a solid understanding of your data's quality will expose your operations to unnecessary risk.
What strategies do you use to ensure data accuracy before deploying AI models?
