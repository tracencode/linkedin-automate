AI drafting capabilities often shine in demos but falter in real-world operations.

I've seen teams excited to leverage AI for drafting documents or generating reports, assuming it's all about creativity. However, letting AI directly write into production data without strict controls is a gamble.
Drafting in a controlled environment is one thing. But in production, where the stakes are high, it’s critical to have layers of checks in place. I prioritize a thorough review process to ensure that the drafts align with business standards and accuracy. Relying on AI alone can introduce errors that lead to compliance issues or operational disruptions.
When empowering AI to draft, I focus on integrating human oversight into the workflow. The combination of AI-generated drafts with human validation creates a balance that enhances productivity while safeguarding against the risks of automation.
