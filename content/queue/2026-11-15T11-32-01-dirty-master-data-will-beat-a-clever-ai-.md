I've seen AI workflows designed to impress but falter with messy data.

Systems can run flawlessly in a lab setting. However, once faced with real-world master data, those clever algorithms often stumble. Last week, a project on invoice automation showcased this truth. The model appeared robust during initial testing, handling clean samples effortlessly. Yet, when we introduced actual operational data—full of inconsistencies like duplicates and missing fields—the performance dropped sharply. I’ve learned that no amount of AI sophistication can compensate for dirty data. That’s why I consistently prioritize data integrity before deploying any AI feature. It’s a recurring theme: tidy up your master data first or risk your AI’s credibility. Have you faced similar challenges in your deployments?
