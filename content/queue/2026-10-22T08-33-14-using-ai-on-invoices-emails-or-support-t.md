I've seen AI solutions pitched as the answer to every operational headache. Recently, a client insisted on using AI to handle their invoice processing. They envisioned a seamless flow from receipt to approval with little human intervention.

The reality? AI can process data, but it can't interpret context. Their invoices came with various formats, line items, and nuances that were unique to each vendor. The AI struggled. Instead of reducing workload, we found ourselves spending more time training the system on edge cases and exceptions.

We had to pause. The most effective solution ended up being a mixture of manual oversight and AI assistance. The team still needed to validate outputs, ensuring accuracy. In the end, we struck a balance between automation and human judgment.

AI is a tool, not a silver bullet. It requires careful integration into existing workflows, especially where human discretion is critical.

How do you decide when AI is the right fit for your operational challenges?
