The latest tools for AI deployment often gloss over essential checks.

Just because a tool like EZClaw promises a quick setup doesn’t mean it’s ready for real operations. I've seen migrations that integrate AI features without adequately validating the underlying data.
The focus tends to be on speed, but the risk of inconsistent or dirty master data can derail an entire project. In fact, I recently halted a migration when it became clear the data integrity wasn't where it needed to be. Ensuring that the data is clean and reliable is non-negotiable if you expect the AI to function effectively.
Before any migration, I look for signs of data accuracy and fitness. If the AI features can’t trust the data they're built on, you have a ticking time bomb on your hands. What checks do you require before integrating AI into your ERP workflows?
