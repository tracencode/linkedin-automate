The latest tools like EZClaw and Allos are making AI deployment sound effortless.

But I can’t stress enough: without proper evaluation sets, you’re risking operational failure.
Recently, I advised a client to implement an AI-driven support ticket system. They were eager to jump in, dazzled by the promise of speed and efficiency. However, we had to hit pause. I insisted on testing with well-structured evaluation sets first. It revealed critical gaps in how the AI understood nuances in customer queries.
This wasn’t just about validation; it was about trust. If an AI solution can’t accurately categorize and respond to tickets, it won’t just look bad—it’ll cost time and money. We refined the model using historical ticket data and established a feedback loop to constantly improve accuracy. After rigorous testing, I finally felt confident in deploying.
A shiny interface doesn’t guarantee success. Proper evaluations do. What’s your experience with deploying AI in real settings?
