I’ve seen automation projects that seemed like a smart move but ended up complicating simple tasks. A couple of months ago, a client wanted to automate their invoice approval process. The idea was to streamline operations and reduce manual handling.

But here’s the catch: automating approvals meant that the system would bypass a critical review step that had been working just fine as a checklist. The team was used to a human touch in validating invoices against delivery notes and past interactions.

Once the automation went live, we quickly faced issues. Invoices slipped through that shouldn’t have, leading to disputes and delays with vendors. What looked like efficiency turned into chaos.

Now we’re rolling back parts of that automation to reintroduce manual checks. Sometimes, keeping it simple is the best approach. Not every process needs to be automated. Sometimes a checklist can do the job better than an AI.

What tasks do you think should remain manual despite the push for automation?
