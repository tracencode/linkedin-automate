In the whirlwind of Odoo migrations, I have a checklist that keeps the chaos at bay. Migrations can seem straightforward, but they can unravel quickly if I miss a step.

First, I assess the data quality. If we’re moving data from an old system, it’s critical to ensure that the master data is clean. Duplicates, inconsistent formats, or incomplete records can create more problems than we started with.

Next, I check the data mappings. Are we confident that the old fields correctly correspond to the new ones? Misalignment here can lead to data loss or errors. An unexpected format change has derailed projects before.

I also consider the historical data. What do we need to carry over? Often, clients want to bring everything, but not all data is relevant. It’s a balance between thoroughness and efficiency.

Finally, I ensure that the new environment is thoroughly tested before going live. A few days of testing can save weeks of troubleshooting down the line.

What’s on your migration checklist that I might have missed?
