Document AI needs to handle real-world chaos, not just neat examples.

Many organizations are excited about deploying AI tools in finance, warehouse, and support roles. These tools promise efficiency and smart decision-making. However, I've found that messy documents can derail even the most sophisticated systems. Last week, a client faced a barrage of invoices, some scanned with stamps and others in different languages. Their expectation was that any document AI could simply read and extract data seamlessly.
The reality is far more complex. If the underlying data is inconsistent—think poor scans, varying formats, or unclear text—no amount of AI sophistication can rectify that. I’ve seen workflows break down when systems are fed garbage in the name of automation.
Designing a robust document AI means preparing not just for clean inputs but for the chaos of real operations. If we don’t account for these variables, we risk undermining our deployments. How do you ensure your document AI can handle the messiness of everyday operations?
