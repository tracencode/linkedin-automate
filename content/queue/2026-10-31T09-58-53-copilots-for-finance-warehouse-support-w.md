The allure of AI copilots can be deceptive.

Many organizations are excited about deploying AI tools in finance, warehouse, and support roles. These tools promise efficiency and smart decision-making. But in practice, not everything should be automated. I refuse to automate tasks that rely on nuanced human judgment. For instance, financial decision-making often requires context that's hard for AI to grasp. Copilots can assist, but they shouldn’t replace critical thinking. The same goes for warehouse management. An AI could forecast demand, but it can’t account for last-minute changes, like supply chain disruptions or customer preferences. When deploying AI in these areas, I focus on augmenting human capabilities instead of substituting them. We need to ensure that AI acts as a tool, not a crutch, especially in high-stakes environments. How have you navigated the balance between automation and human oversight?
