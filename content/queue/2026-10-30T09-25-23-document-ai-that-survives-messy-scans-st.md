Document AI is often hailed for its transformative potential, but its real-world application can be challenging. Just last week, I encountered a situation where a customer needed to automate invoice processing. Their invoices were a mix of scanned documents with varying formats, stamps, and multiple languages. Sounds simple, right?

Not quite. In this case, our existing Document AI struggled with the messy inputs. The OCR accuracy dropped significantly due to inconsistent formatting and language shifts. We had to take a step back and enhance our pre-processing workflows.

Instead of just throwing an advanced model at the problem, we refined our approach to handle the different document types they were using. We focused on improving data extraction, ensuring that we had not just a faster solution, but one that was reliable enough to prevent errors in financial reporting.

It’s a clear reminder that AI must fit the realities of business operations. If it can’t handle the messiness of real-world data, it isn't worth implementing.
