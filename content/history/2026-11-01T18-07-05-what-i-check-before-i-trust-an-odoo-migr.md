The promise of seamless AI integration often masks hidden risks.

Recent tools like Allos and EZClaw make deploying AI agents sound effortless, but I've seen teams overlook critical operational checks in their rush to implement. When migrating Odoo systems to include these new AI features, my team and I focus on three key areas: data integrity, user impact, and process alignment.
First, I evaluate the quality of the master data being migrated. If the data is dirty, any AI feature will struggle, leading to inaccurate results. Second, I consider how these features will affect end users—are they prepared for changes? Lastly, the integration workflow must remain aligned with existing operational processes to avoid chaos.
Skipping these checks can lead to costly mistakes. Have you faced challenges when integrating AI into existing systems?
