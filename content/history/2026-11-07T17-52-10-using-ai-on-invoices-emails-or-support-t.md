The rush to deploy AI often overlooks critical data quality.

Tools like EZClaw and Allos promise quick integration of AI agents into workflows. But I've seen this excitement lead teams down a problematic path. One recent client jumped to automate invoice processing without verifying their data. The consequence? An avalanche of erroneous invoices and support tickets needing manual intervention.
AI can enhance efficiency, but it doesn't magically fix underlying data issues. If your master data is messy, your AI won't perform reliably.  Data quality checks should never be sidelined. We need to ensure the right systems are in place before trusting AI with operational tasks. Otherwise, we risk creating more chaos than efficiency.
