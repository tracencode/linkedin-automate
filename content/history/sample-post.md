Most people do not have a LinkedIn problem.

They have a finishing problem.

They draft a sharp post, then rewrite it until it sounds like a keynote, then never hit publish.

The accounts that grow are usually not the cleverest. They are the ones that show up on a schedule with one useful idea, written the way they talk at work.

If you are waiting for a perfect week of content, you will still be waiting next quarter.

What is the post you have been sitting on?
