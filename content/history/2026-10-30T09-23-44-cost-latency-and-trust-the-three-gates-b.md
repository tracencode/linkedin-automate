There's a lot of buzz around new open-source AI tools like Allos and EZClaw. They promise quick deployment and flexibility, which is attractive. But excitement doesn’t mean they’re ready for production.

I prioritize three gates before releasing any AI feature.

First, cost. I assess the infrastructure and integration expenses. If the AI tool isn’t cost-effective, we’ll burn resources fast. Then, latency. If an AI feature can’t keep up with real-time demands, it’s a hard pass. Users expect seamless experiences, not lagging processes. Lastly, trust. I won’t ship anything unless I’m confident in its accuracy and reliability. Misinformation in production can ruin workflows and erode user faith in the system.

These gates aren’t just checkboxes; they ensure that when we push something out, it’s not just functional but also dependable.
