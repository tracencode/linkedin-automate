Recent tools like EZClaw make deploying AI agents sound simple.

Yet, I see teams get into trouble when they rush these implementations without a solid foundation. A quick fix might look appealing, but I've learned the hard way that sometimes, it’s better to take a step back and evaluate whether a custom module is necessary. Odoo's flexibility allows for configurations that can meet many business needs without complicating the ecosystem.
However, when you integrate AI features, the stakes change. Custom modules can provide better control, especially when working with AI that requires specific data handling or business logic. One size fits all doesn’t cut it; you need to assess data quality, user needs, and the intricacies of your workflows. I’ve witnessed projects fail because of hasty decisions, leading to wasted resources and frustration.
