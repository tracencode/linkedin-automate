The latest AI tools make quick deployment seem easy.

But I’ve learned the hard way that speed can mislead. Many teams rush to configure Odoo or build a custom module without fully understanding their needs.
Take AI agents like those emerging from Allos or EZClaw. They can look like an instant solution. However, if the underlying processes are unclear, you might end up with a patchwork that breaks in production. I’ve seen this firsthand. A hasty integration can lead to messy data and unclear workflows, setting the stage for failure on Monday morning.
A thoughtful approach requires evaluating whether to customize or configure based on real operational demands. Sometimes, sticking with standard configurations can yield a more stable foundation.  In the rush to automate, don’t forget to assess if the AI deployment will enhance existing workflows or complicate them.
