Many AI copilot demos look impressive but fall flat in real-world applications. For a warehouse or finance team, the difference is in practical usability.

In one project, we tested an AI copilot that claimed to assist with inventory management. The demo showcased its ability to analyze data and provide insight. However, in reality, it struggled with the specific terminology and workflows our team used. Instead of enhancing productivity, it added confusion.

In contrast, a tailored AI solution that understood our operations and integrated seamlessly into existing workflows proved invaluable. It allowed the team to pull reports with relevant metrics without needing extensive training. The key was ensuring the AI was designed for the users, not just the demo room.

Next time you evaluate an AI copilot, ask: Will it genuinely fit into the workflow, or is it just a flashy feature?
