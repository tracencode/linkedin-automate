The excitement around AI copilots often overlooks a crucial detail: real-world usability.

A demo might showcase a smooth interaction and instant results, but production environments tell a different story.
In my experience with warehouse operations, the stakes are high. A copilot that can’t handle messy data or unexpected inputs is a liability. I've seen teams deploy shiny AI solutions only to find out they can't manage the realities of fluctuating inventory or incomplete records.
The key difference? A functional copilot must integrate smoothly with existing workflows and adapt to real-time data changes. This isn't just about exploring AI's capabilities; it’s about delivering consistent performance.
What’s your threshold for trusting an AI tool in a critical operational role?
