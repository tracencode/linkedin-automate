Rushing to integrate AI tools often backfires in production.

Recent offerings like EZClaw and Allos promise quick implementations of AI agents, making them seem like the perfect fix for teams looking to streamline processes. However, I’ve witnessed too many teams skip essential evaluation steps in their eagerness to deploy. Just because a tool can be set up in minutes doesn’t mean it will perform reliably under real operational conditions.
I prioritize evaluation sets over immediate deployment. They allow for context-specific training and testing. An AI workflow must go through rigorous checks before entering production. I've seen firsthand how a tool that seemed ready can falter in the chaos of live data. Often, operational failure isn't about the technology itself but how well it’s integrated and evaluated in the context of existing workflows.
