Quick AI deployments often mask deeper issues in data quality.

The recent buzz around tools like Allos and EZClaw may trigger excitement for rapid integration, but I’ve seen enough projects go sideways due to neglected master data. An impressive AI agent means little if it’s fed garbage data.
In one case, we rushed to deploy an AI copilot for invoice processing. It handled the drafting beautifully, but when it encountered variations in our dirty data, it faltered. The missed steps in data cleaning led to errors that not only wasted time but also eroded trust in the system.
Ultimately, AI is only as effective as the quality of input it receives. Relying solely on the promise of quick integration without a solid foundation will lead to pitfalls. Examining and cleaning your master data should be a prerequisite, not an afterthought.
