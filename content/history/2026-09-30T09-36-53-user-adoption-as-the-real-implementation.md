User adoption often gets overlooked in ERP implementations. We focus on the shiny features, the integrations, and the AI capabilities, but the real risk lurks in how users interact with the system.

I’ve seen projects fail not because of technical issues, but because the team didn’t embrace the system. They didn’t understand why certain workflows mattered or how to leverage the new tools.

Take a recent implementation of an Odoo CRM module. We spent weeks customizing it, integrating it with existing systems, and testing every feature. But during training, I realized many users were still attached to their old processes.

They needed to see the value in using the new system — not just in theory, but in real, tangible outcomes. That’s when I knew we had to pivot our strategy. We began focusing on hands-on training and involving users in the customization process.

Now, when we roll out a new feature, it’s not just a demo. It’s about making sure every user feels like they are part of the journey. Without that buy-in, no amount of technology will succeed.

How do you ensure your users adopt the systems you implement?
