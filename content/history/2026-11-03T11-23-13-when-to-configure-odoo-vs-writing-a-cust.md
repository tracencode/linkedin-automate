Many organizations rush to configure Odoo without fully understanding their needs.

The recent excitement around tools like Allos and EZClaw can create a false sense of readiness. Quick AI deployments sound compelling, but the risks often outweigh the benefits. I’ve seen teams dive headfirst into these integrations without accounting for the specificities of their operations.
The decision between configuring Odoo and building a custom module often hinges on the complexity of the workflow. If your needs are straightforward, a configuration might suffice. But when your requirements demand nuanced adaptations, a custom module becomes imperative. By focusing solely on speed, teams overlook critical details like data integrity and user adoption. The aftermath can reveal glaring gaps, especially in production environments where AI integrations must perform flawlessly.
